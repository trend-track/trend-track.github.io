# Trend Track Desk

Trend Track, written TrendTrack on the site itself, is a spy stack for ecommerce: shops, ad libraries, advertisers and email flows under one login.

**Read the full page:** https://trend-track.github.io/

This suits dropshippers, media buyers and DTC operators who spend their week looking at other people's stores and want that research in one tab instead of nine. It is a poor fit for anyone selling a service or software product, because the entire dataset is built around shops, catalogues and ecommerce ads. The caveat is scope creep: eleven features sounds generous until you notice you use two of them, and the plan you need is priced for all eleven. Reachara is the narrower option if competitor ad monitoring and creator discovery are the only parts you were ever going to open.
